Past Lives
(Backgrounds)
Who were you before coming to Cyrene? What path led you here?

Your Past Life represents the life you lived before arriving in Cyrene. It reflects your upbringing, your experiences, and the habits that still influence how you interact with the world.

Unlike your Origin, your Past Life defines who you were—not what you are.

Past Lives may be edited later during character creation, so long as the background you chose remains the same. This serves as a foundation for later systems and allows the game engine to perform necessary checks based on your history.
The Prodigal Child

“You left to find something greater. You return to find what still remains.”


Background Overview:
Born and raised in the lower districts of Cyrene, you grew up among tight alleys, familiar faces, and the unspoken rules of survival. Like many before you, you left the city in search of opportunity—fortune, purpose, or perhaps escape.

But the world beyond did not offer what you hoped. Whether through misfortune, betrayal, or simple bad luck, your journey ended without the success you sought.

Now, you return home.

Cyrene is unchanged… and yet, everything feels different. The streets remember you. The people remember you. And you must decide what your return truly means.


Fast Overview:
Origin: Lower City of Cyrene
Status: Returning Local
Reputation: Known (Mixed)
Wealth: Modest or depleted


Core Traits

Streetwise Survivor
You understand the rhythm of the lower city—the shortcuts, the dangers, and the hidden opportunities.
Gain advantage on checks related to:
• Navigation in urban environments
• Gathering rumors or local information
• Avoiding petty crime or ambushes

Familiar Face
You are not a stranger here.
NPCs in the Lower City are more likely to:
• Recognize you
• Offer small aid or information
• Recall past deeds (good or bad)

Unfinished Story
Your past departure is known—and judged.
At the GM’s discretion:
• Some NPCs may admire your attempt to leave
• Others may see you as a failure or cautionary tale


Advantages

Knows the Streets
You move through Cyrene’s lower districts as if you never left.
• Ignore movement penalties in crowded or tight urban areas
• Identify safe routes, hideouts, and escape paths

Old Connections
People remember you—and some owe you.
Once per session, you may:
• Call in a minor favor from a local contact
• Gain access to restricted or hidden areas
• Secure temporary shelter or resources

Ear to the Ground
You understand the language of the streets—spoken and unspoken.
Gain bonuses to:
• Interpreting slang, codes, or rumors
• Detecting lies or hidden intent among locals

You may convert raw Charisma into practical influence in the Lower City


Disadvantages

Unwelcome Attention
Not all memories are fond.
A figure or faction from your past may:
• Hold a grudge
• Seek repayment of a debt
• Attempt to exploit your return

Distrusted by the Upper City
Your origins—and your failure to “make it”—precede you.
Disadvantage on social checks with:
• Upper City elites
• Officials or aristocrats

Increased scrutiny in formal or guarded environments
Former Corporate Employee

“You climbed higher than most ever will. You saw behind the glass walls. And then—you walked away.”


Background Overview:
Born into privilege in the Upper City of Cyrene, you were raised among influence, expectation, and opportunity. You excelled academically, graduating at the top of your class before securing a coveted position within one of Cyrene’s dominant megacorporations.

For a time, you thrived.

But something changed.

Whether it was ethical conflict, internal politics, or knowledge you were never meant to have—you left. Voluntarily or otherwise.

Now you stand outside the system that once defined you. But the system has not forgotten you.


Starting Position:
Origin: Upper City
Status: Former Insider
Reputation: Educated, Connected, Potential Liability
Wealth: Comfortable (declining)
Primary Theme: Insider knowledge, moral conflict, corporate fallout


Core Traits

Corporate Training
You are disciplined, efficient, and conditioned for high-performance environments.
Gain advantage on:
• Knowledge-based checks (finance, tech, bureaucracy)
• Structured problem-solving and planning
• Interpreting formal documents or contracts

Executive Bearing
You carry yourself with authority and polish.
Gain bonuses to:
• Persuasion in professional or upper-class settings
• Commanding attention in formal environments

System Literacy
You understand how corporations function—on paper and in reality.
You can:
• Identify organizational hierarchies quickly
• Exploit bureaucratic inefficiencies
• Predict corporate responses to crises


Choose Your Corporate Origin

Variant A: ANCR Systems (Hardware Division)
“The answer to all life's problems”

A titan of industrial engineering and physical infrastructure, ANCR Systems dominates manufacturing, robotics, and urban hardware across Cyrene.

Specialized Advantages

Hardware Specialist
You are intimately familiar with ANCR-built systems.
• Automatically recognize ANCR technology on sight
• Gain advantage on repairing or disabling mechanical systems
• Understand industrial machinery
• Reduce time interacting with physical tech (locks, drones, security hardware)

Industrial Contacts
Your former colleagues still operate across factories, depots, and maintenance sectors.
Once per session, you may:
• Secure access to restricted industrial zones
• Acquire tools, parts, or equipment
• Call in a favor from a technician or foreman

Practical Engineering Mindset
You solve problems physically, not theoretically.
• Gain bonuses when improvising with tools, materials, or terrain

Disadvantages

Branded by ANCR
Your departure did not go unnoticed.
• Security systems may flag your identity
• Access may be restricted automatically
• Former supervisors may oppose or attempt to silence you

Rigid Thinking
• Disadvantage on abstract or unconventional problem-solving
• Difficulty adapting outside structured systems

Industrial Reputation
Among workers, you may be seen as:
• A sellout
• A defector
• Someone who “had it easy”


Variant B: Synapse Dynamics (Data & Neural Systems)
“Information is control. We own both.”

Synapse Dynamics leads in data infrastructure, neural tech, and predictive analytics. Their reach extends into communication, surveillance, and cognition itself.

Specialized Advantages

Data Architect
You understand digital systems at a deep level.
• Automatically recognize Synapse software and systems
• Gain advantage on hacking, encryption, and data analysis
• Identify digital vulnerabilities
• Interpret complex data streams quickly

Information Network
Your contacts exist in shadows—analysts, coders, and observers.
You may get opportunities to:
• Obtain sensitive information
• Access restricted databases (temporarily)
• Receive intel on corporate activity

Predictive Insight
You’ve worked with systems designed to anticipate behavior.
• Gain bonuses to reading intentions
• Anticipating enemy actions
• Strategic planning

Disadvantages

Marked by Synapse
You may know too much.
• You may be monitored
• They may attempt to silence, recruit, or reclaim you
• Systems may flag your identity

Detached Perspective
• Disadvantage on emotional or empathetic interactions
• Difficulty building trust with lower-city or non-corporate individuals
The Outsider

“You weren’t born here. That means you see what others ignore—and miss what they take for granted.”


Background Overview:
You’ve heard the stories.

The city of Cyrene—its wealth, its danger, its opportunity. From wherever you come from, Cyrene loomed large in rumor and reputation. And now, for your own reasons, you’ve arrived.

Maybe it’s ambition. Maybe curiosity. Maybe necessity.


Starting Position:
Origin: Outside Cyrene
Status: New Arrival
Reputation: Unknown
Wealth: Varies (often limited liquid assets)


Core Traits

Fresh Perspective
You are not bound by Cyrene’s assumptions.
Gain advantage on:
• Identifying unusual patterns locals overlook
• Questioning norms, traditions, or “obvious” solutions

Unknown Variable
No one knows who you are—yet.
Gain bonuses to:
• Bluffing or creating false identities
• Avoiding detection or reputation-based consequences

You are immune to effects tied to local reputation (until established)

Cultural Gap
You lack familiarity with local customs.
Disadvantage on:
• Understanding slang, etiquette, or unspoken rules
• Navigating social hierarchies without guidance


Choose Your Reason for Arrival

Variant A: Business Traveler
“You’re not here to stay. You’re here to get something done.”

You arrived in Cyrene on official business—assigned by an employer, organization, or distant authority. You have purpose, resources, and a timeline.

Specialized Advantages

Corporate Credentials
You carry documentation and legitimacy.
Gain advantage when:
• Requesting access to formal or restricted areas
• Dealing with officials, clerks, or corporate staff

You may invoke your employer’s authority (within reason)

Expense Account
Your trip is funded—at least partially.

You may:
• Acquire reasonable goods or services without immediate cost
• Secure lodging, transport, or equipment

Excessive use may attract scrutiny

Disadvantages

Accountability
You answer to someone.
Your employer may:
• Contact you with demands or updates
• Penalize failure or deviation

Ignoring objectives may have consequences

Paper Trail
Your presence is documented.
Authorities and corporations can:
• Track your arrival and movements
• Identify your affiliations


Variant B: Sightseer
“You came to see the city. The city may end up seeing you.”

Cyrene was never meant to be permanent—just a destination. A curiosity. A place to visit, experience, and leave behind.

Specialized Advantages

Disarming Innocence
You aren’t seen as a threat.
Gain advantage on:
• Social interactions with civilians
• Gaining sympathy, assistance, or leniency

NPCs may underestimate you

Curious Mind
Everything is new—and you pay attention.
Gain bonuses to:
• Perception and noticing details
• Learning new skills or adapting quickly

Light Footprint
You have no ties here.
Easier to:
• Leave situations behind
• Avoid long-term consequences (initially)

Disadvantages

Naïveté
You don’t know what’s dangerous.
Disadvantage on:
• Detecting scams, cons, or hidden threats
• Interpreting intent in suspicious situations

Targeted
Outsiders attract attention.
You are more likely to be:
• Scammed
• Followed
• Manipulated

No Support Network
You have no one to fall back on.
• Cannot call in favors or contacts (initially)
• Must build relationships from scratch

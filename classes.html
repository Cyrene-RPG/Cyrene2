Kenzai

“Augmentation without discipline is madness. Discipline without augmentation is weakness.”


Class Role The Kenzai is a cybernetically enhanced close-combat specialist, combining martial training with integrated augmentations. They excel in direct engagements, sustained melee combat, and high-risk offensive maneuvers.

Kenzai balance raw combat output with the psychological strain imposed by their augmentations. While capable of extreme lethality, overreliance on cybernetics may result in loss of control.

Key Ability Scores • Strength or Dexterity (primary attack stat, depending on build)
• Constitution (durability and system tolerance)
• Willpower (resistance to Instability effects)

Hit Profile • High durability
• Moderate recovery
• Resistant to pain effects, vulnerable to system disruption (EMP, hacking)

Proficiencies Weapons: All simple and martial weapons, integrated cyber-weapons
Armor: Light and medium armor
Saving Throws: Constitution, Willpower

Skills: Choose 4
• Athletics
• Acrobatics
• Intimidation
• Perception
• Streetwise
• Mechanics
• Endurance
• Insight

Starting Skill Bonuses • +2 Athletics
• +1 Acrobatics
• +1 Intimidation
• +1 to two additional skills of your choice
Core System

Overclock (OC)
Overclock represents how far you are pushing your cybernetics.

Max OC: (suggest: 5–10 depending on scaling)

Gain +1 OC when:
• You deal melee damage
• You take damage
• You defeat an enemy

Spend OC to:
• Activate abilities
• Enter Overclocked state

Combat Resolution
Attack: 1d20 + STR or DEX
Vs target Defense
On hit → deal damage

Overclocked State
You may enter Overclocked at any time.

Cost: 2 OC
Duration: 3 turns

Effects While Overclocked
• Ignore injury penalties
• Reduce incoming damage
• Cannot be interrupted or staggered

Drawbacks While Overclocked

1. Target Drift
At the start of your turn:
Roll 1d20

1–5 → Must attack nearest target
6–20 → Act normally

2. Engagement Lock
You cannot disengage or retreat
You must continue attacking while enemies are present

3. Strain (Escalation System)
Each turn while Overclocked:
Gain +1 Strain

Instability System

When Overclock ends, roll:
Instability Check
1d20 + Strain vs Threshold 10

Results

10+ → Stable — no effect
7–9 → Agitated — disadvantage on next roll
4–6 → Unstable — lose control for 1 turn
1–3 → Cyberpsychosis — lose control for 2 turns

Loss of Control Rules
While unstable or cyberpsychotic:
• You must attack nearest target
• If no enemy → attack nearest entity/object
• You cannot use abilities
• Only basic attacks

Augmented Physiology
• Unarmed strikes count as weapons
• Integrated weapons cannot be disarmed

Close-Quarters Specialist
• No penalties in tight combat
• Gain advantage (or +X bonus) in melee range

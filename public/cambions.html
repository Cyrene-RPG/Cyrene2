Cambions
Cambions are infernal hybrids—beings shaped by the merging of human biology and demonic influence. Unlike many species that emerged during the Reawakening through natural magical evolution, cambions represent a more direct and often more deliberate form of transformation.

Their existence is tied to the intrusion of infernal forces into the modern world. Whether through birth, corruption, or choice, cambions embody the consequences of interacting with entities beyond human understanding.

Physically, they are often marked by pale or altered skin tones, glowing or unnatural eyes, subtle horn growth, and other signs of internal corruption. These features vary depending on the origin of their transformation and the strength of the infernal influence involved.

In society, cambions occupy an uncertain position. Some integrate successfully, using their abilities to secure power or influence, while others are viewed with suspicion or outright hostility. Their presence is a constant reminder that not all changes brought by the Reawakening were accidental.
Common Traits
• Resistance to fire and heat-based damage
• Enhanced vision in low-light and dark environments
• Natural affinity for infernal or forbidden power sources
• Presence that can influence emotional responses in others
Typical Outlook
Cambions often develop a complex relationship with their own identity. Some embrace their infernal nature, viewing it as strength or evolution, while others struggle against it, attempting to retain their humanity.

Their outlook is frequently shaped by how they came into being. Those born into their condition may see it as natural, while those transformed later in life often experience conflict, resentment, or obsession with power.
Physical Description
Cambions retain a largely human form, but their bodies exhibit signs of infernal adaptation. Common features include sharpened teeth, altered eye coloration, faintly glowing veins, and minor horn growth. In stronger cases, these traits become more pronounced, pushing the individual further from a human appearance.

Internally, their physiology is altered to withstand extreme conditions, particularly heat and magical strain. However, this comes with instability—many cambions report heightened emotional intensity, difficulty regulating impulses, or sensitivity to certain types of magic, particularly those aligned with holy or radiant forces.
Cambion Origins
Cambions do not share a single origin. Instead, they emerge through multiple pathways, each producing variations in behavior, appearance, and stability.
Demon-Born
These cambions are the result of direct union between a demon and a human. This can occur through genuine connection, manipulation, or contractual obligation.

Demon-born cambions tend to be the most stable, with abilities that manifest early and develop consistently over time.

Traits:
+1 Additional Fire Resistance
+1 Charisma
Demon-born cambions possess an otherworldly allure, their appearance often rivaling that of elves and other idealized forms.
Cursed
Cursed cambions are created when a human is transformed by external magical forces. This can include spells, witchcraft, cursed artifacts, or exposure to unstable magical environments.

These individuals often experience unpredictable changes and may struggle to control their abilities.

Traits:
-1 Constitution
+2 Magic
Their power is unstable and volatile, often manifesting in unpredictable surges or fluctuations.
Pact-Made
Pact-made cambions originate from agreements between humans and infernal entities. In exchange for power, the individual undergoes gradual transformation.

Over time, the body adapts to the granted power, resulting in physical and psychological changes that align them more closely with infernal beings.

Traits:
+1 Strength
+2 Magic (chosen attribute)
Marked by a sigil granted by their patron, a visible symbol of the power they wield and the entity they serve.

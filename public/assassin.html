Assassins
“Precision without patience is failure. Patience without precision is hesitation.”
Class Role
Assassins are precision operatives who specialize in eliminating targets with speed, efficiency, and minimal exposure. Often enhanced through cybernetic augmentation, they are frequently employed by corporations, gangs, or black-market organizations to carry out tasks others cannot—or will not—handle.

While many assassins operate under structured contracts, freelance operatives exist beyond corporate control, navigating the underworld independently. These individuals often take on high-risk assignments, relying on skill and reputation rather than affiliation.

Assassins excel in stealth, infiltration, and targeted elimination. Their methods vary—some favor close-quarters executions, while others operate from a distance. The most dangerous among them are those capable of seamlessly blending both approaches.
Combat Style
Assassins prioritize positioning, timing, and efficiency over sustained combat. Direct confrontation is rarely their strength—control of the battlefield is.

They exploit weaknesses, strike from unexpected angles, and disengage before retaliation becomes possible. Success is measured not by endurance, but by execution.

Core System
Execution (EX)
Execution represents your ability to identify and exploit vulnerabilities.

Max EX: (suggest 5–10 depending on scaling)

Gain +1 EX when:
• You attack from stealth
• You strike an unaware target
• You critically hit an enemy

Spend EX to:
• Enhance attacks
• Trigger execution abilities
• Enter Execution State
Combat Resolution
Attack: 1d20 + DEX
Vs target Defense
On hit → deal damage

Bonus: Attacks from stealth gain advantage or bonus to hit
Execution State
You may enter Execution State at any time.

Cost: 2 EX
Duration: 2 turns
Effects While in Execution State
• Increased critical hit chance
• Bonus damage against unaware or isolated targets
• Movement does not break stealth immediately
• Attacks deal additional precision damage
Drawbacks While in Execution State
1. Exposure Risk
After attacking:
Roll 1d20

1–6 → You are revealed
7–20 → Remain hidden

2. Fragile Positioning
If attacked while revealed:
• Take increased damage
• Cannot re-enter stealth until next turn

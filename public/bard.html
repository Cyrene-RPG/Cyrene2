Sosenja
“Attention is power. Control it, and you control everything.”
Class Role
Sosenja are performers, entertainers, and social operators who thrive in environments where attention is currency. Whether on stage, in clubs, or embedded within social circles, they use presence, timing, and perception to influence those around them.

Some operate openly, building reputations as artists or public figures. Others move more subtly, using performance as a tool to gain access, gather information, or manipulate outcomes without direct confrontation.

A Sosenja’s strength is not in physical dominance, but in their ability to control perception—shifting the mood of a room, destabilizing enemies, or empowering allies through influence alone.
Combat Style
Sosenja avoid direct combat whenever possible, instead controlling engagements through distraction, influence, and manipulation.

They weaken enemies, enhance allies, and alter battlefield dynamics without needing to engage head-on. Their effectiveness depends on awareness, positioning, and timing rather than raw damage output.

Core System
Influence (IN)
Influence represents your ability to affect others through presence, performance, and psychological manipulation.

Max IN: (suggest 5–10 depending on scaling)

Gain +1 IN when:
• You successfully influence a target
• You perform in front of multiple targets
• You alter enemy behavior or perception

Spend IN to:
• Buff allies
• Debuff enemies
• Disrupt enemy actions
• Enter Influence State
Combat Resolution
Attack: 1d20 + CHA
Vs target Defense
On hit → deal light damage or apply status effect

Bonus: Social or performance-based actions may affect multiple targets
Influence State
You may enter Influence State at any time.

Cost: 2 IN
Duration: 3 turns
Effects While in Influence State
• Allies gain bonuses to rolls
• Enemies suffer penalties to perception or accuracy
• Increased success rate on social effects
• Ability to affect multiple targets simultaneously
Drawbacks While in Influence State
1. Overexposure
At end of turn:
Roll 1d20

1–6 → You become the primary target
7–20 → No effect

2. Mental Strain
• Repeated use reduces effectiveness
• Overuse may cause temporary loss of control

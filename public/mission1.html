Welcome to Cyrene
You are standing on the deck of a ferry, the metal beneath your boots humming softly with the strain of the engines as they push through restless water. The wind feels nice and cool against your face, carrying with it the sharp scent of salt and distant smog—a strange mix of ocean spray and something more artificial. The ferry rises and falls gently with the waves, a slow, rhythmic motion that almost feels calming—if not for the quiet tension hanging in the air around you. It’s late morning, though the light feels muted, filtered through a thin haze that clings to the horizon. As you gaze across the water, the coastline of an island starts to become visible—at first just a faint silhouette, then gradually sharpening into something more defined. Structures begin to take shape—skyscrapers, stacked and sprawling, stretching upward and outward in uneven layers. The skyline of a city reveals itself piece by piece, massive and imposing, like it’s watching you approach just as much as you’re observing it. The closer the ferry moves, the more details emerge—faint glimmers of glass and steel catching the light, dark gaps between structures hinting at depth and density. Even from this distance, the city feels alive… restless… as if something is always moving beneath its surface. Off to your left, a dark-skinned elf leans against the railing, their posture relaxed but distant, as though lost in thought. The wind tugs lightly at their clothing and hair, but they don’t react. Their gaze is fixed on the water below, watching the shifting surface as it reflects fragments of light and shadow. There’s a quiet intensity about them—the kind that suggests they’re not just looking at the water… but thinking about something far beyond it. What do you do?
